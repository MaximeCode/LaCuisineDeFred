Design a **clean, elegant website UI mockup** for a French personal chef and catering service.

PROJECT CONTEXT
The brand is called **“La Cuisine de Fred”**, a local **chef at home and catering service** located near **Chartres, France (Eure-et-Loir)**.
The website must present the chef, showcase culinary creations, explain service offers, and allow clients to request a quote for events (weddings, anniversaries, company meals, etc.).

The tone should feel **convivial, authentic, and artisanal**, reflecting a **home chef who cooks refined but approachable gastronomic cuisine**.

The design must remain **simple, elegant, and professional**, appropriate for a **self-employed artisan chef**, not a large corporate brand.

VISUAL STYLE
Style inspiration: **gastronomic editorial magazine layout**.

Design characteristics:

* Elegant but simple
* Large food photography
* Clean typography
* Editorial composition
* Plenty of white space
* Soft shadows
* Rounded image cards
* Overlapping image cards to create depth

TYPOGRAPHY
Primary font: **Cinzel Medium** for headings.
Use a clean sans-serif font for body text.

COLOR PALETTE
Based on the brand logo colors:

* silver / grey
* dark grey
* black

Add one accent color to enrich the design:

* **wine red / burgundy**

Use wine red mainly for:

* buttons
* CTA highlights
* section accents
* hover states

LAYOUT STYLE
Images should be presented in **rounded cards with soft shadows**.
Food photography cards may slightly **overlap each other** to create a magazine-style composition.

Buttons should be **rounded and elegant**, not overly modern.

GLOBAL CTA
Primary CTA: **“Demander un devis” (Request a quote)**

If possible, place this CTA:

* in the header navigation
  If not, repeat it strategically across sections.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Main navigation:

Accueil
Le Chef
Prestations
Tarifs
Contact

Note: a “prestation” represents an event catering service or a culinary realization.

PAGE 1 — HOME

Hero section:

* Full screen food photography
* Elegant tagline
* Short welcoming text from the chef
* Primary CTA button “Demander un devis”

Tagline example:
“Une cuisine maison et conviviale pour vos événements.”

Editorial composition:
Images of dishes presented as overlapping rounded cards.

Section: Latest prestations
Grid of food images showing recent culinary creations:

* image
* dish title

Section: Key values
Highlight key elements:

* Devis sur mesure
* Produits faits maison
* Produits frais

Use icon + short description cards.

Section: Traiteur à Chartres et en Eure-et-Loir

Short SEO-friendly paragraph explaining that the chef travels around Chartres and the Eure-et-Loir department for events.

Section: Contact CTA
Encourage visitors to request a quote.

PAGE 2 — LE CHEF

Introduce the chef.

Sections:

* Portrait photo
* Story of the chef
* Passion for cooking
* Philosophy: homemade cuisine, fresh products, convivial events

Layout:
Editorial composition with image cards and text blocks.

PAGE 3 — PRESTATIONS

Portfolio of culinary realizations.

Grid of food photography cards with rounded corners.

Categories:

* Mariages
* Événements privés
* Repas d'entreprise
* Produits saisonniers

Each card includes:

* photo
* title of dish or event

PAGE 4 — TARIFS

Section: Catering formulas

Display service packages in elegant cards.

Example formulas:

* Formule complète (apéritif, entrée, plat, fromage, dessert, vin)
* Formule basique (entrée, plat, dessert)

Important note:
“All our dishes are prepared using fresh products. Prices may vary depending on ingredients. Custom quotes available.”

Second section: Seasonal products

Example:
Foie gras variations:

* Nature
* Au Banyuls
* À l’abricot

Also include:
Marinated salmon with lime and ginger.

PAGE 5 — CONTACT

Quote request form.

Fields:

* Nature de l’événement
* Date de la prestation
* Nombre de couverts
* Nom
* Prénom
* Email
* Commentaire

Design:
Clean form layout with clear labels and large input fields.

Add a CTA button:
“Envoyer ma demande”

FOOTER

Include:

* Contact information
* Phone number
* Email
* Service area (Chartres and Eure-et-Loir)
* Link to legal mentions
* Google reviews module placeholder

GENERAL DESIGN GUIDELINES

The site must feel:

* artisanal
* gastronomic
* welcoming
* simple but elegant

Avoid overly modern startup-style UI.
Prioritize **warmth, authenticity, and culinary imagery**.

Use large food photography to drive visual appeal.
